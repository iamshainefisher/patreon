# Practice routines

One 10-minute routine works for every post, and one weekly plan fits a post into seven days. Publish both diagrams as a pinned post, open to all patrons, and link to it from each post's "Try it" block.

## What to focus on in each sub-series

| Sub-series | Scale step (2 min) | Chord step (3 min) | Play-along step (4 min) |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Foundations (F1 to F5) | Play the E major scale and say each note name aloud. | Change between two chords at a time until the change is clean. | Count "1, 2, 3, 4" aloud while you play. |
| Modes (M1 to M8) | Play E major first, then the mode, and listen for the changed notes. | Play the home chord, then the chord that contains the changed note. | Hum or sing the changed note over the sequence. |
| Eras (ER1 to ER11) | Play the scale named in "Mode usage". | Copy the rhythm described in "Character of the sound". | Play along with the audio, then with one of the songs in "Listen". |

## Checkpoints

A patron is ready for the next post when they can do these three things.

- [ ] Play the scale up and down in E without stopping.
- [ ] Change between the chords in time, four beats each.
- [ ] Play the sequence once through in B.
