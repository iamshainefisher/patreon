# Recording guide

Each audio file is about 2 minutes: roughly 1:20 in E, then 0:40 in B. The tempos and feels below are suggested starting points, not fixed rules; the loop counts are worked out from each tempo to hit those lengths.

## Posts without a loop

| Post | What to play |
| --- | --- |
| F1 | The E major scale up and down, slowly, then four bars of an E chord. Repeat in B. |
| F2 | The E major scale up and down three times: once slowly, once pausing on the two half steps, once at a steady pulse. Then B major once. |
| F3 | The seven chords up the scale and back down to E, two bars each. Then the same in B, one bar each. |
| M1 | Four bars of E, four bars of Em, then the E major scale followed by the E minor scale. Repeat the chord pair in B. |

## Checklist for every recording

- [ ] Tune first, and use the same guitar and keyboard sound across a sub-series so posts compare fairly.
- [ ] Record to a click at the tempo above, with one bar of count-in.
- [ ] Leave two seconds of silence between the E section and the B section.
- [ ] End each section on the home chord and let it ring.
- [ ] Name the file with the post code, for example M3-lydian-E-and-B.
