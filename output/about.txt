Theory in E is a weekly music theory series for beginners on guitar and keys.

Every Monday you get one idea, small enough to learn in a sitting: the idea in plain words, the notes, a chord sequence and one thing to try. Every post is in the key of E and ends with the same idea a fifth higher, in B, so you learn to move it anywhere. No reading music needed.

What's in each post

* About 300 words of notes (free)
* A diagram sheet: fretboard, keyboard and chord boxes in E and B (free)
* Two minutes of audio, music only (members)
* Guitar tab (members)


The roadmap:
Part 1, Foundations, from 16 November: the E major scale, chords in E, Nashville numbers, why a fifth higher, and the chords in each mode.
Part 2, Modes, from 4 January: all seven modes, from bright Lydian to unstable Locrian, every one starting on E.
Part 3, Eras, from 8 March: a hundred years of music, from 1920s country blues to today's pop and country.
Part 4, Exercises, from 31 May: arpeggios for every chord in E, then runs that go up each chord and come back down its mode.
Parts 1 to 3 end with a recap and a five-question quiz.
